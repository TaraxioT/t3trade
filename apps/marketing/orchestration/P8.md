# P8: the deliberate phone experience

## Status: COMPLETE (2026-08-22 02:10 IST)

## What the phone shows and why
At <=820px the replica is a fixed-height PINNED MINI-COCKPIT: the mission
capsule (state reel + PnL), the chart card on top, and the agent log as a
capped streaming window below it. The positions table (five cramped
columns), the status bar (its state reel duplicates the capsule's), the
wakeup capsule, the titlebar and the composer do not come along; each drop
is declared in Window.astro / AgentLog.astro next to the layout that cannot
seat it. The kept surfaces step UP one size instead of shrinking: log rows
13px/18 on 13px padding, legends 11.5px, chart mark 30px, chart plot aspect
1000/380 (2.4x the stacked aspect, so the walk has vertical room).
Rationale: at 390px the full console cannot be legible; the chart and the
log are the story, and pinning them means every beat lands on screen
(the target print and the check draw while the reader is looking).

## Pin budgets (deliberate)
- Desktop (unchanged): full-viewport stage, 340vh scroller.
- Phone (<=820): fixed-height window (100dvh - 60px) pinned at top 60px
  for 300vh of scroll, then released. Story completes ~300px BEFORE the
  release (measured: dash reaches 0 at scrollY top+~1550, release at
  top+1748). agy-vision verdict on verify-phone-00..07: "deliberate and
  legible, with a clean start, smooth pinning, and seamless release".
- Unpinned surfaces (821-1080px wide, or <=860px tall): no pin; the log
  card caps at min(520px, 68vh) and streams; pass window base 13% / 0.6.
- Landscape 844x390: NOT pinned (a full-height stage has no room); the
  console is a stacked scroll-through, hero copy tightened, 190px console
  band under the fold (CTA still above the fold there: bottom 384/390).

## Real bugs found and fixed this wave (both affect desktop too)
1. .dip-label had animation-name but no animation-timeline: it ran as a
   0s time animation and was permanently visible ("orphaned -$6.84") at
   every viewport including 1440x900. Fixed in replica.css (added to both
   selector lists).
2. feed-follow translated the overflow box (.log-feed) itself, carrying
   the clip region out of the card: the agent log was EMPTY for most of
   the pinned story on desktop AND phone (debug-desktop-b/c/d/e: EMPTY).
   Fixed via a .log-feed-inner strip inside the clip box (externally
   contributed patch by the developer's wedged fixer agent; reviewed on
   the merits, one defect corrected - the replica.css pass-window
   override still targeted .log-feed - then adopted after behavioral
   verification at both viewports: desktop verify-desktop-a..e
   POPULATED with advancing story, phone verify-phone-01..06 streaming).
3. Reverted an uncommissioned desktop retune that rode in with the park:
   .mission-scroller 240vh -> back to 340vh (the wave-3 baseline).
4. Decontamination (coordinator-steered): tape endpoint back to
   api.hyperliquid-testnet.xyz (mainnet prices under a testnet label is
   safety-adjacent), nav tag back to "testnet · alpha". Grep audit: the
   only remaining "mainnet" strings are the correct "No mainnet support
   of any kind" claim and comments; pre-existing mission.ts comment
   references a Coinbase spot quote as fixture provenance (left as-is,
   flagged for the copy wave).
